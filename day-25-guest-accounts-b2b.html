The concept

B2B collaboration answers a simple question: how does a vendor, auditor, or partner work inside your tenant without becoming your employee? You invite them as a guest user. They keep their own identity (their company email, a Gmail address, whatever they use) and get controlled access to your apps and resources.

How it works:

  1. You send an invitation to their email address.
  2. They click the redemption link and sign in with their own account. If their organization already uses Entra ID, it is seamless; if not, they can use a 1-time passcode sent to their email.
  3. Their account appears in your tenant with user type Guest and a UPN in a distinctive format: vendor.person_contoso.com#EXT#@yourtenant.onmicrosoft.com. The #EXT# in the middle is the exam's favorite tell that an account is a guest.

What guests can do by default: the guest user access setting defaults to "limited access to directory objects." Guests can use the apps and resources you explicitly share, but they cannot browse your full user list or group memberships. You can raise that to member-level access per tenant, but the default is the secure 1 and the exam expects you to know it.

B2B vs B2C, because the exam will try to blur them:

  • B2B (this lesson): you invite outside partners *into* your tenant to collaborate.
  • B2C (Day 266): a separate tenant type for customer-facing apps, where millions of consumers sign up with their own identities. Different product, different tenant.

Guests are managed like any user after redemption: block sign-in, remove from groups, delete. Removing a guest's group memberships or deleting the account revokes their access immediately. For recurring partner access, Access Packages (P2) automate the request-and-approval flow, but invitations are the foundation.

Key vocabulary

  • B2B collaboration: inviting external users into your tenant as guests.
  • Invitation / redemption: you invite by email; the guest redeems by signing in with their own identity.
  • #EXT#: the marker inside a guest's UPN showing it is an external account.
  • Guest user access: the tenant setting controlling what guests can see in the directory. Default: limited.
  • B2C: the separate customer identity product. Not the same as B2B guests.

Try it

Free. You invite a guest and remove the invitation at the end.

In the portal:

  1. In the Microsoft Entra admin center, open Identity > Users > All users > New user > Invite external user.
  2. Enter an email address you control (a second personal email works). Add a short invite message. Send the invite.
  3. Open All users, find the new guest (look for the #EXT# in the UPN), and open it. Note the user type is Guest and the invitation status shows Pending acceptance.
  4. Do not redeem it. Select the guest and Delete to clean up. Deleting a pending invitation revokes it.

Clean up: the pending guest invitation is deleted. No access was ever granted.

Worked example: Meridian Health

Meridian Health hires an external billing auditor for 6 weeks. Instead of creating a member account, the admin sends a B2B invitation to the auditor's firm email. The auditor redeems it with her own company identity, gets added to the "Billing Auditors" security group (Day 26), which has Reader on the billing subscription. She can see invoices and nothing else, and she never appears in the employee directory with member access. On her last day, the admin deletes the guest account and the access ends instantly.

Check your understanding

  1. You see auditor_firm.com#EXT#@meridianhealth.onmicrosoft.com in All users. Member or guest, and how do you know?
  2. A guest needs to use your patient portal app but should not browse the full user directory. Does the default configuration allow this?
  3. What is the difference between B2B and B2C?
  4. An invitation was sent to the wrong email address and is still pending. What do you do?

Exam tip

The #EXT# UPN format is the exam's standard "spot the guest" signal. Pair it with the default limited directory access: guests use what you share, nothing more. And never confuse B2B (partners into your tenant) with B2C (customers in their own tenant type).

Key takeaways

  • B2B guests are external users invited into your tenant; they authenticate with their own identity.
  • Guest UPNs contain #EXT#, for example user_domain.com#EXT#@tenant.onmicrosoft.com.
  • Default guest directory access is limited; shared apps and resources work normally.
  • B2B is partner collaboration in your tenant; B2C is a separate customer identity product.
  • Delete the guest account (or remove group memberships) to revoke access immediately.
